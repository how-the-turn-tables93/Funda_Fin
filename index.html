Assumptions and Notes

  • The parser is tuned for text-based detailed CAS PDFs where folio, scheme, units, cost, and valuation rows are extractable as text.
  • Risk metrics require benchmark history. If the selected benchmark data cannot be fetched from Yahoo Finance, benchmark-sensitive ratios may be unavailable.
  • Scheme matching uses fuzzy text comparison against the AMFI scheme list and may need manual review for very similar plan names.
